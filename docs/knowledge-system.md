# Knowledge system and human interpretation

The verified Intelligence repository identifies Al Brooks’s *Trading Price Action Trends*, *Trading Price Action Trading Ranges* and *Trading Price Action Reversals*; Thomas N. Bulkowski’s *Encyclopedia of Chart Patterns* (2nd edition); and complementary candlestick materials. Versioned manifests, hashes, provenance sidecars, page-referenced notes, research documents, catalogs and decision records preserve source identity and research context.

The codebase contains PDF page extraction and SQLite FTS5 indexing/class-filtered search. However, the latest inspected Intelligence workspace had no populated local index. The project therefore does not claim every book was fully extracted or available to agent retrieval. Document consultation and structured knowledge are different from training an AI model. No fine-tuning, vector database or verified RAG implementation was found.

Visual concepts were not automatically operational. Human explanations resolved ambiguity around context, valid opportunity, timing and causal rules. Candidate implementations were reviewed, backtested, compared, audited, retested, improved or rejected. AI accelerated research and coding; human expertise retained strategic authority.
