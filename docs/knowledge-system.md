# Knowledge system and human interpretation

The documented source set includes Al Brooks's *Trading Price Action Trends*, *Trading Price Action Trading Ranges* and *Trading Price Action Reversals*; Thomas N. Bulkowski's *Encyclopedia of Chart Patterns* (second edition); and complementary candlestick material. The documents and catalogs organize this knowledge for research; they do not train a foundation model.

The inspected Intelligence workspace contained PDF extraction and SQLite FTS5 search utilities but no populated local index. The available evidence does not establish a production retrieval service, vector database or RAG implementation, nor full extraction of every source.

Visual examples leave context, timing and opportunity quality open to interpretation. Human review supplied and corrected those criteria before concepts became measurable candidate rules. Candidates were implemented, backtested, compared, audited and retested; weak or inconsistent results were revised or rejected before portfolio consideration.
