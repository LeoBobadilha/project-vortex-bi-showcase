# Autonomous research pipeline

Project Vortex coordinated scoped work across market research, strategy implementation, historical replay, execution-engine development, risk analysis, audit and prospective-simulation monitoring. Components handed work forward through task records, research outputs, implementation requests, versioned code, test results and recorded decisions. This supported concurrent experiments and reduced repetitive manual implementation.

Agents did not have unrestricted live-order authority. Market-context interpretation, strategic acceptance and any approval to progress toward live capital remained human decisions. Paper Live refers to simulated prospective validation, not real-money operation. The stage sequence is documented in [Research workflow](research-workflow.md).
